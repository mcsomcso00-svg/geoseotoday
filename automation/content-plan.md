## 1. Rationale

- **Why fewer, better**: 3-month GSC = 12 clicks / 1.77k impressions / avg position 33; impressions collapsed after 2026-08-17 and 154 of ~495 URLs are not indexed. Google's own status dashboard lists an **August 2026 spam update starting 2026-08-18** (also a Sep 24 spam update), and Google's AI guide says scaled, non-commodity-free content is exactly what its spam systems target. More volume is the wrong lever; fewer pages with something only we can say is the right one.
- **Selection test (every row passes all four)**: (1) real or demonstrable demand (GSC signal, an official Google/Bing change in 2026, or an evergreen problem the owner is living through); (2) no overlap with an existing `target_keyword` (checked against all 155 zh-Hant files); (3) a citation-worthy asset (table, decision tree, scorecard, regex, checklist, or original test); (4) at least 2 primary sources that were opened on 2026-11-02.
- **Why these topics**: Google shipped a lot in 2026 that our corpus does not cover and that zh-Hant competitors have barely written up: the official generative-AI optimization guide (May 15, 2026), FAQ rich result removal (May 7), Search Console generative AI reports (Jun 3, global by Aug 31), Bing AI Performance (Feb 10; Intents/Citation Share Jun 16), the Googlebot 2 MB fetch limit (Mar 31), preferred sources in AI Mode (May 27), the shared AI-training control (Oct 30), Lighthouse "Agentic Browsing" (Jun 22).
- **Corrects our own corpus**: several existing articles are now stale or at odds with Google's stance (FAQ rich results, llms.txt, chunking). See section 3; those are REFRESHES, not new posts, and ship before or alongside the related new topic.
- **GSC demand handling**: all four zero-click queries map to existing slugs (section 3), so none becomes a new article. They become refreshes with the query term added to H1/H2/intro.
- **Mix**: GEO 5, AI 內容產線 2, On-page & 技術 SEO 6, Off-page & 關鍵字研究 4, 多語言 / 國際 SEO 2, AIO 5 (cluster 3 and 4 deliberately heavy: indexing/spam/crawl is where the site is hurting).
- **Writer rules**: cite the listed primary URLs in-body (Google docs by name + date); never link to a slug dated after the article's own date (all links below are to <= 2026-08-31 corpus; new-to-new links only to an EARLIER row); quote any title/description containing a halfwidth colon; run the date-aware dead-link scan before pushing.
- **Honesty flags** (see section 4): rows 6, 13, 24 need first-party data or manual tests the owner must supply; row 6 publishes internal GSC numbers (owner approval needed).
